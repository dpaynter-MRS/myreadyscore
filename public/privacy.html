Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

My Ready Score collects information you voluntarily provide, including your first name, email address, Quick Ready Score, weakest readiness category, and basic campaign attribution data. We use this information to save your assessment, provide access updates, improve the service, and send preparedness-related email communications you requested.

Analytics and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel to understand visits and actions on our website, measure advertising performance, and improve our campaigns. Meta may receive information such as your browser and device details, IP address, pages visited, and actions such as starting or completing an assessment, submitting the early-access form, or selecting a recommended product. Meta processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can manage advertising preferences through your Meta account and browser privacy settings.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare to host the website and store assessment data, Brevo to manage contacts and send email, and Meta to provide advertising measurement. These providers process information for us or under their own terms.

Affiliate links

Some product links are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, My Ready Score earns from qualifying purchases. Amazon may collect information when you visit its website under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link included in each message. You may request deletion of your stored information by replying to an email from My Ready Score. You may also use browser controls or content-blocking tools to limit tracking technologies.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical safeguards and retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, legal compliance, and legitimate business analysis.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service develops. The effective date above will reflect the latest version.
